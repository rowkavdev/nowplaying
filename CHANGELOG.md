### Added

## [0.1.0] - Unreleased

### Added

- Provider-neutral presence model for Plex, Jellyfin, Navidrome and Emby.
- Self-contained SVG cards with themes, privacy controls and validated embedded artwork.
- Discord Rich Presence formatting, lifecycle, IPC mapping, safe buttons and update controls.
- Provider artwork request, cache, fetch and pixel-bound validation pipeline.
- Documentation for deployment, providers, privacy, customization, architecture and releases.
- Multi-version CI, build smoke artifacts and tag-driven release automation.
